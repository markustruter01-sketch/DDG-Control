## 4. An outsider's view of ddg.limited

I looked at it as a Brisbane business owner who has never heard of DDG.

**What works**
- The first screen is striking, and it says what you sell and that it is cheaper. That was the biggest fault on 17 Nov and it is fixed.
- The prices are plain and public. Almost no agency does this. It builds trust fast.
- "Talk to us in writing" with a five-question form is clear and different.
- The real products in the portfolio are stronger proof than any claim.

**What an outsider trips on**
1. **A whole empty screen after the first one.** On a phone, and on a computer, the second screen is black with one thin line. Many people stop scrolling when a screen looks empty.
2. **The orb is now small on a phone.** After "camera twice as far", the orb takes about a tenth of the screen height. The hero is mostly dark sky. The effect you built is hard to see where most visitors are.
3. **"Press & Hold Orb for Fun" competes with "Get a fixed quote".** A stranger has one clear thing to do, and the page offers a toy above the offer.
4. **"Finance" in the main menu.** A café owner looking for a website taps it and finds trading bots. It reads as a different company. It belongs in the portfolio, not the menu.
5. **The charity panel sits between the portfolio and the prices.** It breaks the path from "what they built" to "what it costs".
6. **Two of the four showpieces say "best viewed on a computer screen".** On a phone that reads as "our best work does not work on your device". (This is the reason for the mobile plan.)
7. **No person.** No name, no face, no "who am I dealing with". The message audit called this the top trust gap. It is still open. An email and a PO box are now shown, which helps.
8. **No client proof.** Fair, there are no clients yet. The founding offer (five places, lower price) is the honest answer to that, and it is not on the page.
9. **The fifth price card sits alone** on a computer screen, with a large empty area beside it.

**What I could not judge:** speed and smoothness on a real phone. Every test I ran was on a machine with no graphics card. This is the biggest unknown about the page. Yesterday added many effects and nobody has measured them. The performance plan (project doc `claude/site-performance-plan-2026-11-18.md`) has not been started.

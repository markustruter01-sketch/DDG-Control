# Australian job boards: which still take web jobs (checked 19 Nov 2026)

| Site | Still running? | Cost to us | Good for | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Upwork** | Yes | 0–15% per contract (about 10%) + $0.15 USD per Connect | All five services; bigger budgets | **Start here** |
| **Freelancer.com** (Sydney company) | Yes | 10% of the job (min $5). Free plan: 6 bids a month. Paid plans from about $5 a month cut the fixed-price fee to 5% | Websites and stores; many low bids | **Second.** Free plan first |
| **Airtasker** | Yes | Service fee taken from the job when it's done; reported 10–20% | Small website jobs; budgets often $300–$1,000 | Websites only, and only at our price |
| **Oneflare** | **No. Closed 30 June 2026**; its traffic now goes to Airtasker | – | – | Skip |
| **ServiceSeeking** | Yes | Subscription plus per-lead credits | Mostly trades; web jobs are few | Skip for now |
| **Fiverr** | Yes | 20% of every order | Fixed "kits" only, never custom work (growth plan channel 4) | Later, once the kits exist |

## Recommendation

1. **Week 1:** Upwork only. Get the profile approved and send the first 10 proposals.
2. **Week 2:** add Freelancer.com on the free plan (6 bids a month). Same templates.
3. **Airtasker:** only answer website tasks with a budget of $1,275 or more.
4. Do not pay for any membership until a channel has sent 10 proposals. Any paid plan is a new cost and needs Markus's yes.

## How the fees change what we keep

On a $1,275 founding-price website:
- Upwork at 10%: we keep about $1,147.
- Freelancer.com at 10%: we keep about $1,147. On a paid plan at 5%: about $1,211.
- Airtasker at 15%: we keep about $1,084.

Sources: Upwork fees and Connects (Upwork help centre, as quoted by gigradar.io and upcat.app, 2026); Freelancer.com fees (aiproposer.com and jobbers.io, 2026); Oneflare closure (whatsthedamage.com.au, and the notice on oneflare.com.au); Airtasker fee range (betterteam.com).

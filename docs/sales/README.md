## The rules (Markus, 17 Nov)

1. Writing only. No calls, no meetings. Skip any job that says it wants a call.
2. New people only. Nobody Markus knows.
3. Only people who have shown they want a build (they posted a job).
4. Listed prices stay. The first five clients get 15% off (founding offer).
5. Never more than two builds at once.

## The weekly rhythm

- **Target:** 10 proposals a week across Upwork and one job board.
- **Each day (about 15 minutes for Markus):** Claude picks the best 2 new jobs, drafts the proposal and a small mock-up. Markus reads, edits if he wants, and sends.
- **Stop rule:** a channel with no paid job after 40 proposals is paused.
- **Count for each channel:** sent, replies, paid jobs, money spent, Markus's minutes.

## Before the first job is accepted

These are still open from the growth plan. A job can be won before they are done, but should not be **started** before them:

- Lawyer: the Build contract (fixed scope, stages, extra work at the hourly rate).
- Insurance: professional indemnity.
- Accountant: GST. DDG is not GST-registered; a few big jobs could cross the $75,000 line.

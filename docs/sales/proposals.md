# Proposals: how we pick jobs and what we send

## Which jobs to answer

Answer a job only if **all** of these are true:

1. It is a website, online store, web platform, app or AI job we already list on ddg.limited.
2. The budget is at least our founding price for that kind of job (website $1,275, store $6,375, platform or app $9,560, AI $3,185), or the post says "open to quotes".
3. It does not ask for a call, a video meeting or "must be available on Zoom".
4. The client has a payment method verified on Upwork.
5. It was posted in the last 48 hours and has fewer than 20 proposals.
6. It is not from anyone Markus knows.

Prefer jobs from Australia and New Zealand (same time zone, AUD), then the UK and US.

## The mock-up step

Every proposal carries one picture: the client's first screen, redesigned by us.

1. Claude reads the job and, if given, the client's current site.
2. Claude makes one first-screen mock-up (computer size) in about 10 minutes.
3. The picture is attached to the proposal. It is labelled "Mock-up by DDG for this proposal".
4. We never use the client's logo or photos without saying "your logo/photos placed here as an example".

## Template C: app, platform or AI assistant

```
Hi [name],

I've attached a mock-up of the main screen as I understand it from your post.

We build platforms like this for ourselves: a private business dashboard (logins, live numbers, AI helpers) and a trading research platform with live charts. So the hard parts (logins, payments, data, AI) are familiar ground.

How I'd approach it:
1. A written scope: every screen and what it does, agreed before any code.
2. A first working version of [the core feature] in [n] weeks.
3. Then [the rest], then launch and a care plan if you want one.

Price: [AUD amount] fixed for the first version, including our founding discount (15% off for our first five clients). Anything outside the written scope is quoted before we do it.

We work in writing: weekly progress notes and a test link you can try any time. No meetings needed.

One question: [the riskiest unknown in their post].

Markus
Digital Dynamics Group · ddg.limited
```

## Checks before Markus presses send

- The price is at or above the founding price, and says "fixed".
- No email address or phone number anywhere (Upwork rule).
- The mock-up is attached and labelled.
- Nothing promised that we have not done (no "100 clients", no reviews we don't have).
- One question at the end, not five.

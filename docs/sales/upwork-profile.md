# Upwork profile: words to paste

Costs (checked 19 Nov 2026, from Upwork's own help pages as quoted by fee guides):
- Free to join. Upwork takes a service fee of **0% to 15% of each contract**, shown before you send the proposal. Plan on about 10%.
- Each proposal costs **Connects**: $0.15 USD each, usually 2 to 16 per proposal. A free account gets 10 a month.
- **Freelancer Plus** is $19.99 USD a month with 100 Connects. Not needed for week one. Decide after 10 proposals. **This is a new cost: your call.**

## Step by step for Markus (about 30 minutes)

1. Sign up at upwork.com as a **freelancer**, using `info@digitaldynamics-group.com`. Turn on 2-step login straight away.
2. Upwork checks your ID. It may ask for a short video ID check. That is Upwork staff, not a client, and it happens once.
3. Paste the sections below. Use a real photo of you: clear face, plain background. Upwork profiles without a face do badly, and the site audit said "no person" is DDG's biggest trust gap.
4. Add the four portfolio items below with screenshots (Claude can make the screenshots).
5. Later, once the first job is won, an **Agency** profile called Digital Dynamics Group can be added. Upwork needs a freelancer profile first.

## Upwork rules to keep in mind

- Do not put an email address or phone number in proposals or messages before a contract starts. Upwork can suspend accounts for it.
- Get paid through Upwork for any client found on Upwork.
- A link to ddg.limited is fine in the profile.
